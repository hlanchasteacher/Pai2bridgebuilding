Phase C — Building the Bridge

Two sessions. Build your bridge, test it, document it, and generate your PDF for Toddle.

1

Step 1 — Construction Plan

Before you touch any material, write your construction plan. This is part of your grade (Criterion C, strand i).

What do you build first, next, last?
What do you use at each step? (scissors, ruler, hands…)
How do you check that each piece measures what it should and that joints hold?
If a piece goes wrong or you run out of material, what do you do? Will you ask for extra material? How much? Why?
2

Step 2 — Go to the supplier (teacher's desk)

Take your Phase B invoice to the teacher's desk and collect your materials. You will receive exactly what you ordered.

You receive exactly what you ordered The sheets and tape from your Phase B invoice. No more, no less.
If you run out, you can ask for extra — but prices have gone up Extra material is charged at a higher price. That extra cost is called the surcharge.

Extra material prices (surcharge)

Material Extra price
Paper — extra A4 sheet 1,500 credits
Tape — extra 10 cm strip 150 credits

Maximum extra: 5 sheets per team (7,500 credits).

How the surcharge is calculated

Surcharge = (extra sheets × 1,500) + (extra tape cm ÷ 10 × 150)

Additional rubric criterion: budget management

This assignment's rubric includes an extra criterion for budget management, scored out of 8. Your total real cost (Phase B invoice + surcharge) determines the score on this criterion. It does not cap your Criterion C mark — it is scored alongside it.
